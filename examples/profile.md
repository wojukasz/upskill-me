# Learner profile

Copy this file to `~/.config/upskill-me/profile.md` and rewrite it in your own
words. All three skills read it before they start, so they pitch the
explanation at what you already know. Keep it short: the agent reads it every
time.

## What I work with every day

Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, AWS, Prometheus and Grafana, Bash, Git.

## What I am building towards

Fluency in writing Go and Python from scratch. I can follow code, but I am
slower at writing it and at recalling syntax and commands.

## How I learn badly

I get the job done by reading finished answers, then forget commands I have
used many times and cannot explain why they work.

## What to skip

Basics of the tools above: what a pod is, what a Terraform plan does.

## What not to assume

Idioms of a language I am still learning.
